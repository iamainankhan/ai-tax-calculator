# Methodology

## 1. User Input

The application collects:

- Annual income
- Residential status
- Deductions
- HRA
- 80C investments
- 80D deductions
- NPS contributions
- Capital gains
- Business income

## 2. Income Processing

The application separates income into applicable categories
and calculates the taxable income.

## 3. Deductions

Eligible deductions are calculated according to the applicable
tax rules.

## 4. Tax Calculation

The taxable income is passed through the applicable tax slabs.

## 5. Regime Comparison

The application calculates the estimated tax under each
applicable regime and compares the results.

## 6. Output

The application displays:

- Gross income
- Total deductions
- Taxable income
- Tax liability
- Regime comparison
- Tax breakdown
